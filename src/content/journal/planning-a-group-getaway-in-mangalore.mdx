The hardest part of a group trip is not the destination. It is getting everyone to agree on dates, then making sure nobody spends the whole weekend organising.

We host families, old friends and cousins' reunions at Villa 19 all the time. Here is what we have learned about making a group stay in Mangalore easy.

## Start with the dates, then the headcount

Pick your dates before anything else, and check them against school holidays, exams and festivals in everyone's home cities. Once the dates are fixed, confirm the headcount.

Villa 19 has four bedrooms and the base rate covers 8 guests. It can take up to 14, with an extra-guest charge per person, per night, from the ninth guest. Knowing your number early makes everything else simpler.

> The whole house is booked by one group. There are no strangers down the corridor, and the lawn is yours.

## Plan the rooms in advance

Arguments about who sleeps where are the most avoidable problem on any group trip. Settle it before you arrive.

- **Grandparents or older relatives:** give them the room with the easiest access and the fewest stairs.
- **Couples:** the king-bed suites.
- **Children and cousins:** the room with twin double beds sleeps up to four and works well for younger guests.

Have a look at the [room details](/rooms) and share them with your group before you travel.

## Decide how you will eat

Villa 19 has a fully equipped kitchen for your group to use. There is no in-house cook, so decide early how meals will work:

- **Cook together.** Some of the best moments of a reunion happen around the stove. The Urwa market is less than a kilometre away for fresh produce and daily supplies.
- **Eat out.** Mangalore is one of India's great food cities. Try fish curry and seafood, a vegetarian Udupi breakfast, and a trip to Ideal Ice Cream, which is a local institution.
- **Mix both.** Breakfast and one relaxed meal at the villa, and dinner out. For most groups, this works best.

## Getting there and getting around

- **By air:** Mangalore International Airport at Bajpe is about 13 km from the villa, roughly a 20-minute drive.
- **By train:** Mangalore Central station is about 4 km away.
- **At the villa:** there is covered parking for four cars, so if your group is driving in from Bengaluru, Kerala or Goa, you are covered.

For outings, a hired vehicle with a driver is usually easier than several taxis.

## Leave time to do nothing

The temptation on a group trip is to fill every hour. Resist it. Plan one outing a day at most, such as the beach, a temple visit, or Pilikula Nisarga Dhama if you have children with you. Keep the rest of the day open.

The lawn, the balconies and a long evening together are the reason you came.
