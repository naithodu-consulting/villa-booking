Villa 19 is not a beach house. It sits in the green, residential neighbourhood of Urwa, with a private lawn, and it is quiet at night. But the coast is close, and two good beaches are a short drive away, with a third worth a half-day trip.

Here are the three we recommend most often, with approximate distances from the villa.

## Panambur Beach

**About 7.6 km, roughly 10 minutes by car**

Panambur is Mangalore's best-known beach, and the one we suggest for a first visit. The sand is wide and well kept, and there is space for children to run about.

**Good for:** families, first-time visitors, and sunset walks.

**Worth knowing:** Weekend evenings get busy. For a calmer visit, go on a weekday or early in the morning. Lifeguards patrol a marked stretch of the beach, so swim only within the flagged area, and only in season.

## Tannirbhavi Beach

**About 8.8 km, roughly 20 minutes by car**

Tannirbhavi is quieter than Panambur and lined with casuarina trees. It feels further from the city than it actually is.

**Good for:** morning walks, couples within a larger group, and anyone who wants less noise.

**Worth knowing:** There are fewer food stalls and facilities here, so carry water and snacks.

## Someshwar Beach

**About 24 km, a longer drive south of the city**

Someshwar is the most dramatic of the three: rocky outcrops meet the sand, and the Someshwar temple stands nearby. It is a beach for looking at more than swimming, and because it is further out, it works best as a planned half-day outing.

**Good for:** photographs, sunsets, and a temple visit on the same outing.

**Worth knowing:** The rocks and currents make it unsuitable for swimming. Stay on the sand and keep a close eye on children.

## A few things we tell every guest

- **The sea is not for swimming during the monsoon.** From June to September, admire the waves from the shore.
- **Go early or late.** The coastal sun is strong between late morning and mid-afternoon, even in winter.
- **Leave the evening free.** After a day at the beach, most groups are happiest back on the villa lawn, with dinner cooking in the kitchen and nowhere else to be.
